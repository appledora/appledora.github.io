🎀 I am skeptical of AI progress that scales everything except accountability. 🎀

I study multimodal learning, guided by four commitments, and each one shows up in my work:

  1. 01Understand, don't just score. Evaluate models honestly, beyond what aggregate metrics are designed to hide.↳arXiv:2505.21649 ↗arXiv:2402.00626 ↗
  2. 02Look inside. Examine the representations behind behavior, and the gap between observed behavior and underlying vulnerabilities or retained knowledge.↳arXiv:2601.10566 ↗
  3. 03Stay efficient. Adapt powerful models without requiring unlimited resources.↳arXiv:2411.16870 ↗arXiv:2603.27383 ↗
  4. 04Ask who it's for. Keep asking who we are building them for.↳arXiv:2510.20043 ↗

Efficiency, interpretability, and inclusion are not separate research agendas for me. They are the same argument at different levels of abstraction.
