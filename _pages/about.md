Hello there!! I'm a CS PhD student at Boston University, trying to understand how multimodal models represent the world and where that understanding quietly falls short. I care a lot about who these models end up working for, so I put a strong emphasis on under-resourced communities, and a good chunk of my **open-source tools and datasets** come from that — see the [projects](/projects) and a partial list of [publications](/publications).

Outside the lab you'll find me with my plants🪴, my pottery ⚱️, or deep in pop-culture 🖖 (some of it lives [here](/misc) ✨). If any of the above sounds like something we should work on together, shoot me an email, or just say hi~
