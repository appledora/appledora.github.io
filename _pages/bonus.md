# Welcome to the BONUS Level! 🎮

If you've made it here, congratulations! You've discovered the secret section where I drop my guard and show you the *real* Nazia. This isn't the polished academic version—this is me, unfiltered, with all my quirks and passions on full display.

## The Story So Far

By day, I'm a PhD student exploring **mechanistic interpretability** and **parameter-efficient adaptation**. By night (and weekends, and basically all the time), I'm a plant parent, pottery enthusiast, and full-blown pop culture nerd.

My research touches **responsible AI, low-resource communities, and vision-language systems**. But what really lights me up is **building tools and resources** that actually help people, and **connecting dots across disciplines**.

## Why This Bonus Level?

The main pages show the professional side—publications, research, the academic journey. But they don't capture the *why*. Why do I care about mechanistic interpretability? Because **understanding systems matters**, whether they're neural networks or ecosystems (or pottery clay).

Why NLP for low-resource languages? Because **access matters**. Why open-source? Because **community matters**.

This page is where those values live. Here's the unfiltered version.
